Importing cat litter to Australia

Do you supply bentonite cat litter to importers in Australia?
Yes. VetPet Care already exports bentonite cat litter to Australia and supplies importers, distributors and wholesalers directly from our factory in India in both 5kg retail and 25kg bulk bags.
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ depends on pack size and whether you need private label. Share your target volume and city, and we send exact MOQ, FOB and CIF pricing within 24 hours.
Do you offer private label or OEM cat litter?
Yes. We offer private label and custom branding on the 25kg bulk and retail bags for qualified distributor partners in Australia.
Which fragrance works best for the Australian market?
All six fragrances — Lavender, Cherry Blossom, Jasmine, Rosemary, Lemon and Non-Scented — are available for Australian import orders, giving distributors a full range across capital-city and regional retail.
Which ports do you ship to in Australia?
We ship from India to major Australian ports including Port Botany (Sydney) and Port of Melbourne. Exact freight terms and timelines are confirmed with your quote.
Are there biosecurity requirements for importing cat litter into Australia?
Mineral products like bentonite clay can be subject to Australian biosecurity import conditions. We provide a Certificate of Analysis, Safety Data Sheet and other export documentation your customs broker needs, but buyers should confirm current requirements with their broker or the Australian Department of Agriculture before shipping.
