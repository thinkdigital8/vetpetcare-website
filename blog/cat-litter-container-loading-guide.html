Cat Litter Container Loading Guide: How Many Bags Fit in a 20ft or 40ft Container?

By VetPet Care Published 3 Sep 2026 7 min read

Before you can compare landed cost across suppliers, you need a working number for how much cat litter actually fits in a container. Here's a practical answer for importers planning a full container load (FCL) order, and why cat litter behaves differently from lighter cargo.

Quick answer: A 20ft container typically carries around 1,000-1,100 bags of 25kg bentonite cat litter (roughly 26-28 tons), because weight limits are reached before the container's volume is full. A 40ft container rarely carries proportionally more of a dense product like cat litter for the same reason — always confirm exact figures with your supplier for your specific pack size and destination.

Why weight, not volume, limits cat litter containers

Bentonite cat litter is a dense, granular mineral product. Unlike bulky, lightweight cargo (think foam packaging or empty bottles), a cat litter shipment typically hits the container's legal road-transport weight limit long before the container's physical volume is full. That's why cat litter is usually described as a "weight-out" cargo rather than a "cube-out" cargo — the number that matters most is tons per container, not cubic meters.

This matters directly for your sourcing decision: a bigger container doesn't automatically mean proportionally more product if the cargo is dense, which changes how you should compare freight quotes between 20ft and 40ft options.

20ft vs 40ft: which should you book?

Since weight — not volume — is usually the limiting factor for cat litter, the practical guidance is:

  • Choose 20ft if your order is a single-product cat litter shipment. You'll reach the weight limit well before filling a 40ft container's extra volume, so the smaller box is typically the more cost-efficient booking.
  • Consider 40ft only if you're combining cat litter with lighter, bulkier products in the same shipment (mixed-SKU orders), where the extra volume genuinely gets used.

If you're unsure, ask your supplier for a loading plan comparing both options against your actual pack size mix and order volume — a direct manufacturer can usually turn this around quickly.

Documents you'll need for an FCL import

  • Commercial invoice — itemized value of the shipment for customs.
  • Packing list — bag counts, pack sizes, carton/pallet breakdown and container weight.
  • Bill of lading — the shipping line's contract of carriage and title document.
  • Certificate of origin — confirms the goods were manufactured in India, sometimes required for preferential duty treatment.
  • Fumigation certificate — required by many countries when wood pallets are used in the container.

Your destination country may have additional product-specific import requirements — check with your customs broker or ask your supplier what documentation they typically prepare for shipments to your market.

How to plan your first container order

  1. Decide your pack size mix. Pure 25kg bulk maximizes weight efficiency; a mix with 5kg retail bags adds flexibility for shelf-ready stock.
  2. Confirm your destination port and any local weight restrictions. Some countries apply stricter road-weight limits than others.
  3. Request a loading plan and FOB/CIF quote based on your confirmed pack size mix and container choice.
  4. Review documentation requirements for your market before the container ships, not after it arrives.
  5. Book and track — a supplier with export experience should be able to share vessel schedule and estimated transit time.

Frequently asked questions

How many 25kg bags of cat litter fit in a 20ft container?
Roughly 1,000 to 1,100 bags of 25kg cat litter, depending on palletizing and packaging weight, since a 20ft container is generally limited to around 26-28 tons of payload by road weight regulations rather than by its internal volume.
Is a 40ft container better value for cat litter?
Not usually on its own. Cat litter is a dense product, so a 20ft container typically reaches its weight limit before its volume is full. A 40ft container has roughly double the internal volume but a similar or only slightly higher weight allowance, so it rarely carries proportionally more cat litter unless combined with lighter cargo.
Can I mix 5kg and 25kg bags in the same container?
Yes. Most manufacturers can combine 5kg retail bags and 25kg bulk bags in a single container to match both your retail shelf needs and bulk/wholesale volume in one shipment.
What documents are needed to import a container of cat litter?
Typically a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and certificate of origin, plus any product-specific or wood-pallet fumigation certificates required by your destination country's customs authority.
