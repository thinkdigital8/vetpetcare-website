How Much Cat Litter Do You Need Per Month? A Usage & Cost Guide

By VetPet Care Published 25 Oct 2026 6 min read

Buy too little and you're making extra store runs; buy too much and bags sit around losing freshness. Most new cat owners have no real sense of how much litter a cat actually gets through in a month - and the honest answer depends more on your habits than your cat's.

Quick answer: One cat typically uses about one 5kg bag of clumping bentonite litter per month with daily scooping and a full change every 3-4 weeks. Multi-cat households don't scale in a straight line - budget roughly 1.5 to 2 times more litter per additional cat, since odor control forces more frequent full changes, not just bigger boxes.

Usage for a single-cat household

With daily scooping and a full litter change every 3 to 4 weeks, most single-cat households get through roughly one 5kg bag of clumping bentonite litter per month. That assumes a standard litter box filled to the recommended 4-inch (10cm) depth, which is the depth that lets clumps form fully instead of sticking to the tray base.

Non-clumping litter tends to run out faster in practical terms, since the whole box needs replacing more frequently rather than just topping up around scooped clumps.

Usage for multi-cat households

Multi-cat usage doesn't scale as a simple multiple of the single-cat number. Odor and waste concentration build up faster with more cats sharing boxes, which pushes full changes to roughly every 1-2 weeks instead of every 3-4 — meaning two cats typically use closer to 1.5-2x a single cat's monthly total, not exactly double, and three cats land somewhere around 2.5-3x rather than triple.

The standard box-count rule — one litter box per cat, plus one extra — also affects usage, since more boxes in use means more total fill volume even before accounting for faster change cycles.

What actually changes your usage

  • Fill depth. Filling below the recommended 4 inches saves litter short-term but weakens clumping, often costing more in wasted, poorly-formed clumps.
  • Scooping frequency. Scooping once versus twice daily affects how long a fill lasts before a full change is needed, especially in multi-cat homes.
  • Box size and count. Larger boxes and more boxes both increase total fill volume, independent of cat count.
  • Litter type. Clumping litter generally stretches further than non-clumping, since you're removing only soiled portions rather than the whole fill each time.

Cost per month: bulk vs retail bags

For most single-cat households, a 5kg retail bag matches monthly usage closely enough that bulk buying mainly saves on trips to the store rather than meaningfully reducing cost per use. For multi-cat households, shelters, or catteries working through 15-25kg a month, a 25kg bulk bag typically offers a noticeably lower cost per kilogram than buying multiple retail bags — the trade-off is needing dry, sealed storage space for the larger bag between uses (see our storage and shelf-life guide for how to store an opened bulk bag properly).

How to get more out of every bag

  • Fill to the recommended depth, not less. Under-filling causes weak clumps that fall apart during scooping, wasting more litter than it saves.
  • Scoop before clumps break down. Leaving waste in too long can cause clumps to soften and spread into surrounding litter, contaminating more of the fill than necessary.
  • Choose a genuinely strong-clumping formula. A formula with higher water absorption forms firmer, more completely removable clumps, leaving more clean litter behind after each scoop.
  • Store opened bags properly. Resealing bags and keeping them dry prevents pre-absorbed moisture from weakening clumping before you've even used it.

Frequently asked questions

How much cat litter does one cat use per month?
A single cat typically goes through one 5kg bag of clumping bentonite litter every 3 to 4 weeks with daily scooping and a full monthly change, though this varies with box size, fill depth, and how often you scoop.
Does clumping litter cost more per month than non-clumping litter?
Clumping litter usually costs more per bag but less per month in practice, since you scoop out only the soiled clumps and top up rather than replacing the entire box as often as non-clumping litter typically requires.
How much litter do I need for two or three cats?
Usage doesn't scale perfectly linearly since multi-cat households need more frequent full changes to manage odor, not just more boxes. As a rough guide, budget for roughly 1.5 to 2 times the single-cat amount per additional cat rather than a flat multiple.
Is buying a 25kg bulk bag cheaper than multiple 5kg bags?
Generally yes - bulk bags typically offer a lower cost per kilogram than retail-sized bags, making them worthwhile for multi-cat households, shelters, or anyone willing to store a larger bag between uses.
