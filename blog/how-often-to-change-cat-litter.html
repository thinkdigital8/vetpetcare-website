How Often Should You Really Change Cat Litter?

By VetPet Care Updated 28 Aug 2026 6 min read

Daily scooping keeps odour down, but a full litter box change follows a different rhythm. Change too often and you waste litter; change too rarely and odour and hygiene suffer. Here's how to get the timing right with clumping bentonite litter.

Quick answer: With daily scooping, clumping bentonite litter — including VetPet Care's formula — is usually fully emptied, cleaned and refilled every 3 to 4 weeks for a single cat. Multi-cat homes need more frequent full changes.

Daily scooping vs full replacement

There are two separate jobs, and confusing them is where most people go wrong:

  • Daily scooping — remove solid waste and urine clumps every day with a slotted scoop. This is what actually controls odour day to day.
  • Full replacement — empty the entire tray, wash it, dry it and refill with fresh litter. This happens far less often, on a roughly monthly cycle.

Because clumping litter lets you lift out only the soiled portion, the clean litter around it stays usable — which is exactly why full changes can be spaced weeks apart when you scoop consistently.

These are general guidelines. The right frequency depends on the number of cats, box size, ventilation and the individual cat's habits.

Signs it's time to change

Don't just watch the calendar — watch the box. Do a full change sooner if you notice:

  • Odour lingering even right after scooping
  • Clumps starting to break apart instead of holding firm
  • Litter looking damp, sticky or unusually dusty
  • Your cat hesitating to use the box, or going elsewhere

Tips to make litter last longer

  • Use the right depth. Fill to about 4 inches (10 cm). Too shallow and clumps stick to the base; too deep just wastes litter.
  • Scoop every day. Consistent scooping is the single biggest factor in how long a fill stays fresh.
  • Top up, don't overfill. Add a little fresh litter after scooping to keep the level steady.
  • Choose a quality clumping formula. Strong, tight clumps hold together on removal so less clean litter is lost. VetPet Care's 99% dust-free bentonite is built for exactly this.

Frequently asked questions

How often should you change clumping cat litter completely?
With daily scooping, clumping bentonite litter is typically fully emptied, cleaned and refilled every 3–4 weeks for a single cat. Multi-cat homes need more frequent full changes.
Do you need to scoop cat litter every day?
Yes. Daily scooping of solid waste and clumps keeps odour down and extends how long the litter stays fresh between full changes.
What are the signs it's time to change the litter?
Lingering odour after scooping, clumps breaking apart, litter looking damp or dusty, or your cat avoiding the box are all signs it's time for a full change.
How much litter should you put in the tray?
Fill the litter box to a depth of about 4 inches (10 cm). Too shallow and clumps stick to the base; too deep wastes litter.
