Clumping vs Non-Clumping Cat Litter: What Bulk Buyers Should Know

By VetPet Care Updated 28 Aug 2026 7 min read

For importers and retailers, the choice between clumping and non-clumping cat litter is not just about performance in the litter box — it affects freight weight, shelf turnover, margins and how often customers come back to buy. This guide breaks down the practical differences so bulk buyers can stock the right product.

What clumping and non-clumping litter actually are

Clumping cat litter is usually made from sodium bentonite clay. When it meets liquid, it swells and binds into a firm clump around the waste. The owner scoops out only the soiled clump and leaves the rest of the tray clean and usable.

Non-clumping litter — often calcium bentonite, silica or recycled paper — absorbs moisture but does not form a solid, removable clump. It controls odour for a while, but because waste spreads through the tray, the entire box has to be emptied and refilled more frequently.

Quick answer: Clumping bentonite litter forms solid clumps around waste on contact, letting owners scoop only the soiled portion. Non-clumping litter must be fully replaced more often. For most retailers, clumping litter drives better repeat purchases.

What sells better on retail shelves

Repeat purchase is where clumping litter wins. Because it is quicker to clean and controls odour longer, cat owners are more satisfied and reorder the same product. For a pet shop or supermarket, a product with a higher reorder rate is worth more than a slightly cheaper bag that customers abandon after one try.

Freight and packaging considerations

Bentonite is dense, so ocean and land freight are priced on weight. The verdict for importers

For most retail and distribution channels, clumping bentonite litter is the stronger commercial choice: better odour control, a higher reorder rate and premium positioning. Non-clumping still has a place in budget segments and for owners with specific preferences, but if you are building a range around repeat customers, lead with a quality clumping product.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between clumping and non-clumping cat litter?
Clumping litter, usually sodium bentonite, forms a solid clump around waste on contact so only the soiled portion is scooped out. Non-clumping litter absorbs moisture but does not bind it, so the whole tray must be changed more often.
Which cat litter is better for retailers to stock?
Clumping bentonite litter typically drives higher repeat purchases because it is easier to maintain, controls odour better and lasts longer per bag — improving customer satisfaction and reorder rates.
Does clumping litter weigh more for freight?
Bentonite clumping litter is dense, so freight is calculated on weight. Buyers offset this with efficient 25kg export bags and container-optimised loading, while the longer usable life per bag improves cost-per-use for end customers.
