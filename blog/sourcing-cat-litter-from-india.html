Sourcing Cat Litter from India: A Quick Guide for Importers

By VetPet Care Updated 28 Aug 2026 8 min read

India has become a competitive sourcing hub for bentonite cat litter. For importers weighing suppliers across Asia, this guide covers why India is attractive, what to check before you commit, and the commercial terms to line up.

Quick answer: India's bentonite reserves and manufacturing costs make it an increasingly attractive sourcing base for clumping cat litter — good quality at competitive prices, with export-ready 5kg and 25kg packaging and private-label options.

Why source from India

Three factors make India a strong option for cat litter sourcing:

  • Raw material. India holds natural bentonite clay reserves, the core ingredient in premium clumping litter.
  • Manufacturing cost. Competitive in-house processing keeps factory pricing attractive at scale.
  • Logistics reach. For nearby markets such as Bangladesh and Vietnam, lead times can be shorter than sourcing from Europe or China.

VetPet Care, for example, has shipped 320+ tons of bentonite cat litter to eight countries — the USA, UK, Bangladesh, Vietnam, France, Italy, UAE and Australia — directly from its production facility in India.

Understanding MOQ, FOB and CIF

Three terms shape every export quote:

  • MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) — the smallest order the supplier will produce, usually tied to pack size and whether you need private label.
  • FOB (Free On Board) — price with the goods loaded at the origin port; you arrange onward freight and insurance.
  • CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) — price including sea freight and insurance to your destination port.

Ask for both FOB and CIF so you can compare landed cost accurately against local sources.

Private label and packaging

If you're building your own brand, confirm the supplier offers private label and custom branding on both retail and bulk bags. Also check that retail packaging is shelf-ready — a zip pouch with a tear notch, a clear usage panel and batch tracking all help sell-through. VetPet Care's 5kg pouches and 25kg export bags are built for exactly this split between shelf and container.

How to start an enquiry

To get an accurate quote quickly, share four things: your destination country and port, target monthly volume, preferred pack size (5kg / 25kg) and fragrances, and whether you need private label. With that, a supplier can return MOQ, FOB and CIF pricing and arrange samples.
