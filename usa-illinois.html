Illinois bentonite cat litter — FAQ

How does litter reach Illinois if it's landlocked?

Containers land at a coastal port (LA/Long Beach or NY/NJ) and rail into Chicago's intermodal terminals — one of the world's largest rail hubs — for fast Midwest distribution.

Do you supply Illinois and Chicago distributors?

Yes — importers, distributors, private-label brands and retailers across Illinois and the Midwest can source 5 kg retail and 25 kg bulk bentonite litter from us.

Can you private-label for an Illinois brand?

Yes. We provide OEM/private-label bentonite litter with your artwork and UPC barcodes, export-documented for Midwest retail and e-commerce.

What is the minimum order for Illinois?

Orders ship by full container load routed to Chicago via rail. Share your volume and timeline for a factory-direct quote.
